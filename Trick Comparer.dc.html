Three open decisions

Each is an alternative to how 1a handles it. Say which you want and I'll fold it into the prototype.

1bControls on a right rail

Always reachable under the thumb holding the phone, and it never covers the trick. Costs about a sixth of the width permanently, and the trim timeline still has to live somewhere.

1cLibrary grouped by tag, not searched
backflip · 12
+9
round-off · 5
+3

Every tag becomes a shelf you scroll sideways, so comparing tries of one trick is one row away. Clips with several tags appear on several shelves, which can feel like duplicates.

1dLock by dragging the two tracks together
drag together to lock

The gesture shows what locking means — two windows becoming one — instead of a button whose effect you have to remember. Harder to find the first time, and it needs a visible unlock affordance.
